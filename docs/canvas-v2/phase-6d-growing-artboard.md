# Canvas V2 Phase 6D — growing premium North Star artboard

Phase 6D replaces the fixed slide with a geometrically growing editorial
surface. The artboard begins at a generous 1680 × 945 minimum, then measures
the complete rendered document after fonts and evidence settle. The iframe and
outer artboard resize to those natural bounds in either axis. The iframe never
scrolls; the dotted world canvas owns pan, zoom, and fit-artboard behavior.

Very large compositions remain visually intact. The user-facing artboard keeps
its authored dimensions while only the PNG supplied for model observation is
proportionally bounded to a 4096px edge and twelve megapixels.

The initial inner starter card is removed. A new canvas opens as a continuous
clean-white North Star artboard. V2-native design grammar establishes the
proven V1 visual language without importing any V1 code:

- clean warm-white surface and generous negative space;
- direct-on-surface editorial composition;
- fine rules and restrained violet/blue accents;
- cards and filled containers only with communicative justification;
- 170px app identity lanes;
- complete unwrapped screenshot sequences at approximately 235px height;
- 18px sequence rhythm, natural aspect ratio, and subtle evidence shadows.

The model still owns all composition and communication decisions. These are
product defaults and vocabulary, not a hidden layout or repair engine.
