# Northstar Phase 3.5 — Premium Design Intelligence

Phase 3.5 makes design intelligence executable and measurable without turning Northstar into a template system.

## Outcome

Every creative source response now includes:

- one problem-specific governing visual idea;
- a structural novelty signature;
- an evidence-to-narrative plan;
- grounded analytical intents where the evidence supports them;
- executable cumulative HTML, CSS, SVG, and safe JavaScript that realizes the plan.

The browser—not the model's prose—verifies that required narrative beats, communication roles, analytical forms, focal hierarchy, evidence bindings, and readable text exist in the rendered artboard. Operationally safe work may remain visible while creative revision continues, but it cannot be published as premium work until the measured contract and independent quality floor pass.

## Runtime flow

1. The creative model observes the exact accepted source and rendered pixels.
2. It authors `designIntelligence` and `sourceEdit` in one response, preserving time to first transformation.
3. Deterministic sanitation validates evidence identities, semantic completeness, and structural novelty against recent artboards.
4. The same mounted browser applies the candidate behind the existing atomic validation shield.
5. The browser reports premium-contract realization alongside geometry, evidence survival, assets, and collision facts.
6. The author critic revises from the exact render.
7. An isolated reviewer applies the same universal publication floor at Low, Medium, and High.
8. Publication remains a metadata transition on the exact accepted browser revision.

## Novelty

Novelty is not a random color seed. Each completed direction persists a structural signature covering:

- information topology;
- dominant geometry;
- reading path;
- medium combination;
- title integration;
- evidence treatment;
- signature behavior.

The next run receives recent signatures and must materially diverge. Exact fingerprints and structurally similar repetitions are rejected before browser dispatch. The semantic contract does not prescribe the replacement form.

The mounted browser also derives a second fingerprint from quantized rendered geometry, narrative roles, focal priority, analytical presence, and evidence treatment. A paraphrased design plan therefore cannot publish the same realized information structure under different words.

## Premium narrative contract

Each artifact declares its own required narrative beats. The only universal communication roles are thesis, evidence, and resolution. Problem-specific work may add comparison, analysis, relationship, recommendation, decision, risk, next step, provenance, context, metrics, or annotations.

Required beats are bound to visible DOM nodes with:

- `data-ns-narrative-beat-id`
- `data-ns-communication-role`
- `data-ns-visual-priority`

Required analytical intents use:

- `data-ns-analysis-id`
- `data-ns-source-ids`

The browser reports missing beats, roles, analyses, unresolved analytical sources, focal count, minimum readable text, evidence-role diversity, and repeated-container pressure.

## Universal quality floor

The isolated reviewer scores:

- information hierarchy;
- evidence legibility;
- analytical depth;
- structural originality;
- composition coherence;
- craft precision;
- decision usefulness.

Publication cannot pass when any dimension falls below its Phase 3.5 threshold, even if the model returns `publicationReady: true`. Thinking depth changes exploration and persistence only.

## Diagnostics

Canvas diagnostics now exposes:

- premium contract state;
- narrative and required-analysis realization;
- structural novelty similarity;
- minimum readable text size;
- design fingerprint;
- independent minimum quality score and universal quality verdict.

The full event stream retains the exact blocker and advisory lists for each accepted source stage.
