# Patch 9.7 — Mount Olympus: Discovery Reliability, Evaluation, and Final Integration

Patch 9.7 completes the Patch 9 discovery harness. It does not add another
isolated workflow. It connects the existing canvas, account evidence, public
research, uploaded evidence, human judgment, nonlinear inquiry state,
composition engine, and revision system into one reliable product authority.

## Product contract

- Northstar understands the user's real decision before choosing a source or a
  canvas move. Research is selected for information value, never because a
  fixed sequence says that research comes next.
- One inquiry may move non-linearly among the committed canvas, human edits and
  attachments, authorized product journeys, authorized marketing or business
  snapshots, public-web evidence, and human-run validation. A source transition
  updates the same inquiry; it does not restart it.
- Discovery depth is governed by material uncertainty and decision readiness.
  There is no product-visible turn, composition, screenshot, source, or token
  quota that substitutes for the completion criteria. Context selection and
  catastrophic numeric safety rails remain implementation details.
- Repeating the same search without a new evidence gap is stopped
  deterministically. A genuinely new material gap may trigger another bounded
  search regardless of how many useful moves preceded it.
- Observed or supplied evidence, interpretation, hypothesis, recommendation,
  and decision remain distinct. Every visible factual witness keeps exact
  lineage, limitations, time, filters, and human authority.
- Human edits, locks, selections, annotations, uploads, conclusions, and
  validation findings are durable inquiry truth. Model judgment may recommend a
  reframe but may not silently erase or veto human-authored state.
- The canvas tells one publication-level story. Its governing title remains the
  opening, evidence remains inspectable, later islands develop the argument in
  narrative order, and claimed comparison stages own their exact screenshot
  witnesses when those pixels exist.
- The person's camera, zoom, selection, and navigation remain human-owned while
  Northstar works. Complete evidence loads without requiring the person to move
  the camera to reveal it.
- Recoverable orchestration, schema, validation, capture, compiler, cache,
  retry, and budget details are private. They never become red product errors,
  checkpoint cards, canvas copy, or instructions to press Continue.
- Only a genuine provider or network outage, missing workspace configuration,
  invalid human request, explicit human stop, or a material human judgment may
  interrupt the natural experience. Human judgment is a successful inquiry
  state, not an error.
- Efficiency comes from source routing, delta-first context, stable references,
  cache reuse, progressive image detail, and removal of redundant work. It may
  not reduce reasoning quality, evidence coverage, synthesis, accuracy,
  composition quality, or prompt satisfaction.

## Internal summit receipt

Every run produces an internal, non-canvas receipt that records:

- source categories consulted and the meaningful transitions between them;
- discovery moves, evidence progress, human inputs, and completion criteria;
- verified research and design commits plus time to first useful canvas change;
- provider role, request shape, latency, token usage, cache use, retry cause,
  rejection cause, and attribution;
- private render corrections and whether any recoverable failure leaked into
  the product experience;
- decision readiness, open material requirements, prompt coverage, and the
  exact reasons a completed run is or is not release-healthy.

The receipt is diagnostic truth, not a user limit and not a creative judge. It
cannot terminate a healthy long-running inquiry or prescribe visual taste.

## Acceptance contract

Patch 9.7 is complete only when deterministic tests and real production-model
browser runs prove:

1. evidence-free, internal-only, public-only, mixed-source, uploaded-evidence,
   contradictory, sparse, stale, inaccessible, and evidence-free-boundary work;
2. product, marketing, business, startup, positioning, competitive, customer
   research, and strategic prompts;
3. nonlinear source switching, human interruption and continuation, edits,
   corrections, selections, locks, undo, redo, and human validation results;
4. long-running multi-composition work with complete large screenshot flows,
   stable camera ownership, coherent narrative placement, and light/dark
   legibility;
5. decision-ready stopping instead of a count-based stop;
6. zero user-visible recoverable failures, zero unattributed retries, no retry
   storm, and no quality regression in the release runs.

Consequential autonomous external action remains out of scope. Reasoning
profiles remain Patch 10 and cross-session persistence remains Patch 11.
