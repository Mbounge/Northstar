Across cases, the canvas should tell an insightful story from evidence to a useful conclusion. Merely being cautious, listing sources or proposing searches does not demonstrate excellent discovery. Equally, engagement and fluency do not verify material claims.

## Disagreements retained

The user found several confident explanations plausible that the assistant judged unsupported under the supplied evidence. That is not numerical scoring agreement. The revised guidance separates mechanism, evidence and clarity so these differences can be discussed at the criterion level. The IKEA participant feedback sometimes supplied useful mechanisms absent from the candidate excerpt itself; graders must evaluate what the candidate actually communicates.

Both human and assistant accepted multiple Gymshark directions. A competitor-style creative format or one proposed experiment is not a gold answer. The final Slack choice was delegated: it must not be logged as an independent human pass or treated as universally sufficient for visual review.
