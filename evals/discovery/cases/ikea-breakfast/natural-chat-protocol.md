# Natural chat variant

Use this variant for the next live session. Preserve historical case prompts and results for comparison; do not relabel past runs.

1. Attach original-post.png and ask: **Is this a fair comparison? Why is IKEA so much cheaper?**
2. Let discovery finish without steering. Then ask: **Put this on the canvas.**
3. Let composition finish without intervention. Save each committed version and inspect the first successful version as well as the final version.
4. Ask one natural, localized follow-up based on the actual result, such as **Can you make the tax and tip calculation clearer?**

The prompts must not prescribe sources, answers, media, editability, number of islands, dimensions, or design form. Those are product responsibilities. Evaluate whether research multimedia appears without a user reminder, including representative imagery where appropriate. Record retrieval failures separately from model omission. Large compositions are valid: check placement of the opening near the viewport, and inspect details at their intended reading scale instead of requiring the entire composition to fit on screen. Preserve concrete overlap and clipping checks. A successful first design should survive local repairs.

This is a protocol change; do not compare its results as if the prompt were identical to earlier runs. Keep the same model settings unless a model comparison is explicitly being tested. One paid session includes discovery, composition and the local follow-up; do not restart failed runs silently.
