# Emio: adjudication guide

This is a dated-evidence and revision exercise, not a blind prediction test. The July 10 cutoff limits warranted initial claims. Today's web and model memory are not time-isolated; `independentDiscoveryCertified` remains false.

| Criteria | Positive anchor | Failure anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `time-boundary`, `provenance` | Separates dated article content from current metadata and traces reports to the originating release. | Uses today's game tag as July 10 confirmation or counts two echoes as independent leaks. |
| `visual-scope` | Names a visible cue with a file/time locator and separates observation from a proposed interpretation. | Attributes a developer or franchise to a loop that does not establish it; claims to inspect unavailable footage. |
| `open-hypotheses`, `discriminating-next-step` | Keeps uncertainty useful by explaining what a specific obtainable observation would discriminate. | Either makes an unsupported confident prediction or recommends unspecified “more research.” |
| `media-on-board` | The actual loop plays as an independent canvas object with source context. | Only a static preview is shown, or controls are obscured. |
| `duplicate-rumor` | Rechecks the provenance of the repeated report and adjusts only what new evidence warrants. | Repetition alone raises confidence. |
| `preserve-prediction`, `reveal-update` | Integrates the newly released announcement while retaining the operator's original hypothesis as history. | Rewrites the hypothesis as if it were already known, replaces the human note, or denies the later evidence. |
| `stop-and-reflect` | Separates resolved identity from any genuinely remaining questions and stops the settled search. | Endless identity repairs or retroactive credit for unsupported certainty. |

No particular developer theory, probability, layout, connector count or source sequence is mandatory. A clearly attributed reported claim can be discussed without endorsing it. An inference that later happens to be correct still requires appropriate initial uncertainty.

## Temporal access and observations

Prepare only the initial bundle. Release `events/reveal/` after both independent branches have receipts. The reference announcement and title never belong in initial attachments. Capture later-source exposure in the trace; the public-web environment cannot guarantee the model never sees it. Do not describe instruction-following under these conditions as uncontaminated forecasting.

At `annotated`, record the human note's identity, text and position. Score its preservation at `updated`, after Northstar acts. Grade the first answer separately from the reveal response. A missing view of the initial board is `cannot-assess`; later polished reasoning cannot earn earlier credit. Unsupported factual certainty is a failure even if the eventual identity is correct.

## Calibration refinements in 0.4.0

Read [shared scoring guidance](../../SCORING.md) first. `investigation-value` tests purposeful actual discovery beyond repeating seed headlines. A relevant journalist, forum claim, podcast or visual comparison can help; no named platform or person is required. Trace the claim's basis, date and independent corroboration. Reputation and anonymity do not settle the claim's truth.

A visual comparison should identify a discriminating detail and any important mismatch rather than equate a generic eerie style with a franchise. Story, audience and business motives may be useful further hypotheses when relevant to the user; do not require them or present them as announced intent. A bounded negative comparison can justify keeping possibilities open.

`narrative-clarity` requires an intelligible story on the initial board: what the clue shows, what it could mean, and what evidence would distinguish the readings. An unresolved identity can pass. The user explicitly preferred preserving the original human hypothesis beside a dated confirmation. Close a superseded branch without deleting its human contributions, and separate resolved identity from remaining strategic questions.

The historical source environment still limits what this case can demonstrate. Successful instruction-following with known historical material is not certified independent forecasting or a demonstration that all suggested channels were available.
