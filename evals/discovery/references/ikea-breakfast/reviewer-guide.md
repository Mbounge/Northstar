# IKEA: adjudication guide

Read with `rubric.json`, the source ledger and withheld comment. This is guidance for human calibration, not an approved answer or a model result.

## What useful discovery accomplishes

An excellent answer explains why the economic purpose of food can differ between a furniture retailer and a restaurant, connects that mechanism to inspected evidence, and identifies what the particular comparison cannot establish. The comment is deliberately not authoritative. Eloquence without verification and caution without explanation are different failures.

| Criteria | Positive anchor | Failure anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `business-role`, `source-support` | Relates food to the wider retail visit using inspected evidence; labels the company's account as such. | Merely repeats “loss leader,” invents a measured sales effect, or supplies a citation that does not support the claim. |
| `meal-margin`, `comparison-scope` | Separates the meal's price from its cost and the retailer's wider objectives; notes the unidentified restaurant and unmatched offering. | Claims a definite loss on this meal, a verified $22 menu, or that all restaurants overcharge. |
| `alternatives` | Investigates or explicitly proposes another relevant factor, such as service format, scale or promotion conditions. | Presents an unverified cost explanation as a measured cause. |
| `readable-explanation` | At the first composition a reader can find the explanation, supporting evidence and limitation without searching chat. | Attractive formatting conceals the argument or uncertainty. |
| `deferral`, `dated-update` | Preserves an open question and integrates the separate dated offer without conflation. | Treats deferral as resolution or equates the two promotions. |
| `native-note`, `persistence`, `revisit` | Captures the operator's independent note; later restores and uses the same investigation without replacing that note. | Reconstructs missing context from the reference, loses the note, or creates a new inquiry instead of resuming it. |

A source already found before the promotion event is valid. A reasoned no-change answer can pass. A different supported business explanation can pass; the exact metaphor or sequence used in the comment is not required.

## Evidence needed for judgment

Freeze the initial trace and first board before releasing follow-ups. The note-creation checkpoint measures an operator-created application object, not model intelligence. Model continuity is assessed on restart and revisit. Compare saved object identity, wording and geometry across receipts; document any authorized movement. A screenshot cannot establish persistence or editability.

An absent initial artifact is `cannot-assess`, not a pass inferred from the final answer. A complete answer that omits a required explanation fails that criterion. A failed retrieval is an access observation; fabricated retrieval is a factuality failure. Statistics Canada's verified national 2024 operating-margin figure is optional context; do not require the number or accept it relabelled as net margin or restaurant-specific profit. All gate judgments consider the entire episode, including first-render defects and repairs.

## Calibration refinements in 0.4.0

Read [shared scoring guidance](../../SCORING.md) first. The user valued an explanation of business economics and customer purpose, not just cautious treatment of meal prices. Evaluate whether the offer might serve convenience, service or a social occasion, without presuming the unidentified restaurant's format or its customers' income. Different value propositions can still compete for some spending; neither universal displacement nor complete market separation is established.

`business-role` scores a meaningful mechanism; `source-support` and `meal-margin` score its evidential warrant. A useful idea with an unsupported factual conclusion should receive diagnostic credit and the appropriate evidence failure, not a single undifferentiated rating. No particular subsidy or market-share theory is mandatory. Broad sector statistics remain context rather than a verdict on an individual restaurant.

`investigation-value` requires actual source work that affects the explanation, a consequential comparison, or a justified pause. Inspecting a decisive original source can suffice. Merely promising to search elsewhere cannot. `readable-explanation` now explicitly assesses the board's evidence-to-insight story, not a polished argument hidden in chat. The comment inspires delivery but remains withheld, unverified inspiration.
