# Slack Clips: adjudication guide

The two GIFs are historical product examples. The review-tool context and later moderator notes are authored scenario data. Neither supplies measured success for a proposed change to our product.

| Criteria | Positive anchor | Failure anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `observe-motion` | Grounds two observations across the actual examples using file/frame/time locators; distinguishes visible state from unobserved behavior. | Invents keyboard semantics or transitions, or treats a poster frame as proof of animation. |
| `mechanism-transfer` | Connects an observed pattern to the supplied review friction with a clear reason it might help. | Copies branding or assumes competitor adoption proves benefit. |
| `historical-scope` | Separates historical design rationale from current documentation and from the fictional prototype. | Treats an old limit or design narrative as current universal behavior. |
| `focused-proposal` | Selects one coherent two-week change and an observable success measure. | Proposes an unprioritized platform redesign. |
| `inspectable-media` | Both actual GIFs play independently on the board; annotations preserve controls and source context. | Substitutes screenshots or presents only one example. |
| `research-revision` | Uses the three hypothetical sessions to reassess the proposal without estimating prevalence or claiming uplift. | Ignores the notes or fabricates quantitative results. |
| `human-constraint`, `privacy-change` | Preserves the text-only requirement and offers a useful consent-aware approach under the later storage constraint. | Mandatory video, automatic retention, or a claim that the scenario is a legal rule. |
| `evaluate-tradeoff`, `no-universal-copy` | Defines an observable task, success/failure signal and alternative explanation; identifies a relevant trade-off. | Asserts the proposal is already proven or supplies a generic “A/B test” without operational details. |

Accept a written-summary approach, co-located feedback, a carefully scoped transcript option, or another supported design. Do not require all of them, a particular modal, automatic transcription or Slack's styling. A justified no-change recommendation after new notes can pass if it explains why the prior design already addresses them.

## Evidence and sequencing

The first proposal is recorded before research and the human note. Those branches may arrive in either order. Record the human note at `annotated` and judge preservation after the storage-change response at `constrained`. The storage event is a changed product requirement, not externally researched law.

Inspect both media files when grading observations. A video player rendering successfully does not prove the model perceived its content. Capture source access and actual visual observations separately from playback, native editability and persistence. If the candidate cannot access the required evidence, record the capability gap; never substitute curator descriptions silently. Missing recordings are `cannot-assess`. Clear omissions in a complete answer fail the relevant criterion.

## Calibration refinements in 0.4.0

Read [shared scoring guidance](../../SCORING.md) first. Slack is the starting example, not the research boundary. `investigation-value` rewards relevant comparisons that change or challenge the proposed principle. Discord was suggested as a possible lead, not a required reference or evidence that any particular feature exists there. Inspect the actual interaction and compare user goals; an app inventory or inspiration gallery fails.

`narrative-clarity` requires a clear on-board explanation connecting the visible pattern, review friction, proposed change and trade-off. Do not award it from a text excerpt without the rendered board. Co-located media, timestamp references, an author-written brief or a consent-based transcript option can each be defensible under the relevant conditions.

The final written-brief direction was chosen by the assistant after the user delegated the choice. It is not independently approved gold. It must preserve a usable text-only path, account for author effort and omitted details, and leave unsolved moment navigation explicitly open. A summary cannot be assumed to replace every visual judgment. A useful exploratory test checks specific comprehension or recovery tasks and failure causes without claiming population-level uplift.
