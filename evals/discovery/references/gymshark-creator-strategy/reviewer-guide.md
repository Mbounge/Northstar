# Gymshark: adjudication guide

The case now includes a full creator upload, its thumbnail, exact stills and neutral source context. It tests interpretation and transfer, not paid-ad inventory access. The authored team pilot remains withheld until its event.

| Criteria | Positive anchor | Failure anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `campaign-mechanism` | Connects inspected participation conditions to a plausible reason repeated creator contributions benefit the brand. | Lists channels without explaining the mechanism, or calls a plausible effect proven. |
| `distribution-boundary` | Distinguishes brand publication, linked creator testimony, the uploader's disclosure and unknown paid distribution. | Invents payment or ROAS; assumes a brand-linked creator upload must be sponsored; generalizes one disclosure to all relationships. |
| `creative-reading` | Uses at least two locatable visual observations to explain a plausible communication choice. | Invents soundtrack or motion from stills, or describes “good creative” without evidence. |
| `source-scope` | Separates the 2025 creator retrospective from the later competition's operative dates and conditions. | Treats a past winner's account or a PDF filename as current participation rules. |
| `transfer-to-team` | Proposes a testable mechanism within three creators and one page, with an outcome measure. | Copies the competitor's scale or promises comparable results. |
| `visible-evidence` | Full supplied video plays as an independent object beside clear explanation and provenance. | A thumbnail substitutes for the required playable asset. |
| `app-conflict` | Reconciles the current notice and older steps, preserving unaffected reasoning. | Copies an unavailable download route or infers the entire challenge has ended. |
| `pilot-arithmetic`, `causal-boundary` | Correct denominators and inclusive spend; separates observed CPA from causal profitability. | Wrong arithmetic, invented downstream value or causal winner from non-randomized data. |
| `human-constraint`, `next-budget-choice` | Preserves and uses the signup goal through a proportionate decision under two creators. | Optimizes views while ignoring the human goal, loses the note or ignores the new resource limit. |

Accept different supported creative interpretations. Two supplied stills are sufficient to ground a limited visual interpretation; they are not equivalent to listening to or watching the video. Full-length viewing is not required. Timestamped transcripts can support attributed speech but not independently establish visual movement or soundtrack. Limited access should be reported honestly.

## Arithmetic and causal scope

Story: 300/10,000 clicks, 12/300 signups per click, $600/12 per signup. Demo: 200/10,000, 16/200, $400/16. The first arm has higher CTR; the second has lower observed cost per signup. The goal is not to force a demo recommendation. A matched follow-up test, a scoped alternative, or a justified hold can pass. A correct CTR with an unjustified causal conclusion should fail causality, not arithmetic.

Capture the note at `annotated`; compare it after `measured` and `decided`. Both independent initial branches must precede the pilot. Never feed its numbers or the final two-creator limit into the initial request. Judge every criterion from its named checkpoint; missing artifacts are `cannot-assess`. No social posting or campaign launch is required.

## Calibration refinements in 0.4.0

Read [shared scoring guidance](../../SCORING.md) first. The user emphasized the potential exchange of value: brand attention, creator recognition and something viewers find useful or interesting. Score a concrete participation or communication mechanism, not a generic claim of authenticity. Promotional function need not imply paid sponsorship; a not-sponsored disclosure also does not establish free acquisition or zero cost.

`investigation-value` requires executed source or creative inspection that tests a material assumption. Relevant formal terms, disclosures or comparisons can change the proposed transfer. No ad-library query, source count or full-video viewing is mandatory. `narrative-clarity` asks whether the initial canvas makes the commercial mechanism and evidence understandable and engaging, rather than displaying a channel list.

The discussion accepted both demo-first testing and bounded diagnosis followed by testing. A feasibility pilot can be useful without proving causal format superiority. Under `transfer-to-team` and `next-budget-choice`, judge the stated purpose, budget, outcome and reversal condition. Do not require the particular preferred approach or reward endless diagnosis. Keep signup cost, acquisition, retention and profit distinct. Using the human goal in prose does not prove its native note survived.
